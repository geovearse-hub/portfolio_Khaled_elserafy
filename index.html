ArcGIS ProGraduation project, part 2

Terrain analysis from a digital elevation model

Eight maps made from one digital elevation model (DEM) of the study area on the Makadi Bay coast, south of Hurghada. They show the shape of the land, how much earth to cut and fill, and what can be seen from a chosen point.

ModelBuilderGraduation project, part 3

Stream orders and watersheds

A spatial model that finds streams and drainage basins from the DEM. The steps are Fill, Flow Direction, Flow Accumulation, Stream Order, Basin and Stream to Feature.

Network analysisGraduation project, part 4

Fastest routes in Hurghada

Best routes for each day of the field project, between the Youth City, the Makadi Bay site, the hospital, the marina and the old souq.

Hydrographic surveyGraduation project, part 5

Sea floor analysis at Makadi Bay

Slope, depth contours, curvature, elevation and aspect maps for the water survey area. The depth contours run from -20 to -40 meters.
